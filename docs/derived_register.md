# Derived register

Schwab does not send these. Each one has one producer. The full inventory of
older derived values is still the queued register; this file starts with the
values added for the confluence matrix.

| Value | Producer | Why Schwab does not send it |
|---|---|---|
| Per-strike composite | `confluence_composite.score_magnitudes` | A rank of one expiry's book, from 0 up to that preset's ceiling. Each factor in the score is its absolute magnitude, scaled by a Hyndman-Fan type 7 percentile from the 5th to the 95th across the strikes that have it, then multiplied by its weight. A required factor that is missing blanks that strike. The top strike is not rescaled to 100. Schwab sends the contracts, not a score. One call per expiry inside `_ranked_matrices`, when the book is priced. The matrix request reads that ranking. |
| Per-strike volatility blend | `confluence_composite.iv_by_strike` | Schwab sends `volatility` on each contract. The column is one number for the strike, weighted by that contract's open interest plus its volume. A contract missing either count is not weighted. When the weights sum to zero, the blend is the simple mean of the contracts that have a volatility. When none do, the factor is missing. One call per expiry inside `compute_terrain`. The matrix reads the expiry it shows. |
| Per-strike vanna | `math_exposure_core.compute_exposures_by_strike` | Schwab does not send vanna. One Black-Scholes vanna (`math_exposure_core.bs_vanna_array`, every contract at once) per contract, from that contract's Schwab volatility, inside the one exposure book. The matrix reads the strike's `net_vanna`. |
| Day-over-day move of the call wall, put wall and gamma flip | `terrain_engine.level_moves` | Schwab sends no option level. Each level today less the prior market day's own, both from the one levels producer (`compute_terrain`: today's published levels, and the prior day's stored capture at its own spot and time, `server._prior_day`). Unknown without both days. It replaces the gamma-weighted average strike and its 0.15 threshold, which had no source (operator 2026-11-11). |
| Call wall and put wall | `math_exposure_core.pick_gamma_wall_strikes` | Schwab sends no option level. The call wall is the strike holding the most call gamma exposure in dollars per 1% move, the put wall the one holding the most put gamma exposure, over every strike of the book, whichever side of the price each sits on (SpotGamma's definition; operator 2026-11-11: "lets go with spot gamma"). Both may be one strike, and a wall may sit through the price. Unknown without a price. |
| Net call gamma peak and net put gamma peak | `math_exposure_core.pick_volatility_point_strikes` | Schwab sends no option level. Call gamma exposure less put gamma exposure at each strike: the net call gamma peak is the strike where that is most positive, the net put gamma peak the one where it is most negative. A book with no strike on a side has no peak on that side. Once labeled LVP and HVP (operator 2026-11-11: "name hvp and lvp something that is more meaingful"). |
| Per-strike charm | `math_levels.compute_charm_by_strike` | Schwab does not send charm. One Black-Scholes charm (`math_exposure_core.bs_charm_array`, every contract of the expiry at once) per contract. A contract that cannot be priced and has not settled leaves its side and the strike's net unknown. One call per expiry inside `terrain_engine.compute_terrain`. The full-book charm map is those expiry maps added. The matrix reads the expiry it shows. When that publication is the newer banked capture, the forces side sums read this map. A banked capture this publication did not price is summed by the same function, once, at that capture's valuation instant. When the front expiry is listed today, the same-day rail reads that expiry's map. A later front expiry's is computed by this function at the time to today's close, once, in `terrain_engine.same_day_levels`. Sign: net = call − put; a positive net is a dealer book gaining delta as time passes, which the dealer hedges by selling the underlying (Haug, The Complete Guide to Option Pricing Formulas, §2.2). |
| ATR, daily, weekly, monthly | `terrain_atr.compute_atrs` (`math_volatility.compute_atr`) | Schwab sends candles, not an average true range. Wilder's ATR(14) (Wilder 1978, New Concepts in Technical Trading Systems): TR = max(high − low, \|high − prior close\|, \|low − prior close\|); the first ATR is the simple mean of the first 14 TRs, each later one (ATR₋₁ × 13 + TR) / 14. Daily on Schwab's daily candles with today's as Schwab pushes it (LEVELONE_EQUITIES open, high, low and the regular session's last, `server._todays_candles`; not derived); weekly and monthly on Schwab's own weekly and monthly candles (/pricehistory, as sent), the current period as of Schwab's newest answer. Fewer than 14 TRs: none. |
| A chart bar Schwab has not sent whole: the forming 5-, 15- or 30-minute bar, and every 3- and 60-minute bar | `server.aggregate_bars` (called by `server.chart_bars`) | Schwab's /pricehistory sends 1-, 5-, 15- and 30-minute bars once each is complete, and no 3- or 60-minute bar; the streamer sends the 1-minute bar alone (Schwab Streamer API §5.1 CHART_EQUITY). The bar is Schwab's 1-minute bars of its span: the first open, the highest high, the lowest low, the last close, and the volumes added when every minute sent one. A completed 5-, 15- or 30-minute bar is Schwab's own. |
| Gamma flip, GSF, GRC (the gamma profile) | `math_levels.compute_gamma_profile`, `gamma_flip_from_profile`, `compute_gamma_support_levels` | Schwab sends each contract's gamma at its own price, not at other prices. The book's dealer gamma (+call / −put) × open interest × multiplier × S² × 1%, each contract's gamma recomputed at each of 241 prices across ±15% of the price: the Black-Scholes-Merton gamma e^(−qT)·φ(d1)/(S·σ·√T), d1 = (ln(S/K) + (r − q + σ²/2)T)/(σ√T) (Hull, Options, Futures and Other Derivatives, ch. 19), with σ Schwab's `volatility`, r the chain's `interestRate` (Schwab's /chains OptionChain, `docs/schwab/schwab_market_data_api_spec.pdf` p. 27) and q its `dividendYield` (sent on every /chains answer, not in the spec's schema), percents as sent, and T the one time to expiry (`time_et.time_to_expiry_years`). Schwab also pushes a Dividend Yield with the stock's quote (LEVELONE_EQUITIES field 23, `docs/schwab/schwab_streamer_api.pdf`): another field with another value (2026-11-12: QQQ 0.445 pushed, 0.434 on its chain), read by nothing; the options are priced with the rate and yield sent with their own chain (operator 2026-11-12: "i agree with your recomendation"; `tests/test_gamma_flip_schwab_rate_v1.py`). A contract sent without its rate or dividend yield is not priced (`flip_diag["unpriced"]["no_rate"]`). The flip is the linear crossing of zero nearest the price (SqueezeMetrics, SpotGamma); GSF and GRC are where the curve falls to half its value at the price, below and above. |
| Same-day levels (0DTE) | `terrain_engine.same_day_levels` | Schwab sends no 0DTE field (operator 2026-11-09: "schwab does not have a 0DTE field that is one we compute ... we must have one producer at the source to compute all levels for all tickers on a 0dte basis"). The whole book's own producers read on the nearest listed expiry alone. An expiry listed today: its exposure books from Schwab's gamma and delta as sent, its gamma profile and its charm map. None listed today: the next listed expiry priced as if it expired at today's option close (`math_levels.repriced_to_close`): each contract's Black-Scholes-Merton gamma and delta (`math_exposure_core.bsm_gamma_delta`; Hull ch. 19) on Schwab's `volatility`, `interestRate` and `dividendYield` as sent, at the time from the valuation instant to that close (`time_et.time_to_expiry_years`, its 10-minute floor included); its gamma profile and charm at that same time. A contract that cannot be priced leaves its Greeks unknown, never Schwab's Greek at its own expiry. After today's close a chain is valued at that close (`time_et.valuation_instant`), so the levels stand as priced there, a later expiry's at the 10-minute floor. Absent when the chain lists no expiry from today on and, for a later expiry, when the chain is priced on a day with no option window held (a weekend, a holiday, or Schwab's /markets answer for today not held). |
| Gross gamma | `confluence_composite.magnitudes` | Call leg plus put leg of the book's 1% gamma, and only when both legs are zero or positive. A negative leg leaves the cell empty. Not scored. |
| Put/call gamma, put/call volume, volume/open interest | `confluence_composite.magnitudes` | Ratios of the book's own legs. Blank when the divisor is zero or unknown. Not scored. |
| Vanna in dollars per vol point | `confluence_composite.magnitudes` | The book's per-strike `net_vanna` times the book's spot. The book stores `net_vanna` before that multiply. |
| Strike distance | `confluence_composite.at_price` | The strike minus the live price, and the row the live price sits under. The ranking is the book's; the price beside it is live. |
| Score slope | `confluence_composite.build_matrix` | `100 × weight / (p95 − p5)` for each scored factor that varies. A diagnostic, not a second score. |
| Preset and ceiling | `confluence_composite.preset_name`, `confluence_composite.ceiling` | The weight row for that expiry's days to expiration: 0, 1–7, or 8 and beyond. The ceiling is 100 times the weights that are in the score. Flow and the change in gamma stay at zero, so they do not raise it. |
| Heat color | `confluence_composite.build_matrix` (each column's `peaks`: its largest absolute value on the expiry), the row's numbers by `confluence_composite._shades`; drawn by the page's one intensity rule, `EdShell.strength` and `EdShell.heatFill` (`static/js/ed-core.js`) | Each cell's strength is its number divided by its column's peak, capped at 1, a straight proportion; the color runs from the theme's zero toward its positive or negative heat color by that strength. The composite uses the score against the ceiling. Put/call columns shade by 1 − put/call (served `pc_gex_lean`, `pc_vol_lean`): positive call-heavy, negative put-heavy. Every heatmap and bar on the page draws by the same rule. |
| Thin book | `confluence_composite.build_matrix` | Fewer than 10 strikes in the expiry have open interest or volume. The score is still computed. The note is served as text. |
| Flat factor | `confluence_composite.score_magnitudes` | Every strike with open interest or volume has the factor, and the 95th percentile equals the 5th, so that factor adds nothing. A factor missing on any of those strikes is not called flat. The note is served as text. |
| Level price on the rail | `server._screen_levels` | Each level is sent at its own price. The page draws that price on the rail between the strike rows. A price outside the visible strikes is a button above or below the matrix. The page does not move the price onto a strike. |
| One-day move | `server.expected_move` | The live price plus and minus the book's implied one-day move. Schwab sends neither price. The levels panel (`server.levels`: `em_up`, `em_dn`) and the confluence markers carry it. Not scored. A strike between those two prices is marked `inside` by `server._inside_move`. |
| Premium of an option trade print | `app.options.order_flow.history.tape_row` | Schwab sends a print's price and size (LEVELONE_OPTIONS LAST_PRICE, LAST_SIZE) and the contract's `multiplier` (/chains), not their product. Price × size × the chain record's multiplier, every digit kept; unknown when the print carries no size or the record no multiplier. The Options Flow tape's Premium column. |
| Where an option trade print landed | `app.options.order_flow.history.quoted` | Schwab sends no aggressor side and no trade condition on LEVELONE_OPTIONS. The print's price against the contract's bid and ask held when it was received: `at_bid`, `at_ask`, `inside_spread`, `outside_spread_low` (below the bid), `outside_spread_high` (above the ask); `unknown` without a bid or an ask. A comparison, never a buy or a sell. The Options Flow tape's "vs Market" column. |
| Book imbalance, flat | `app.options.order_flow.engine._readout` (`_book_imbalance_from_totals`) | Schwab sends each level's TOTAL_VOLUME, not a ratio. (bid − ask) / (bid + ask) over the best 1, 3 or 5 levels of a side, with those totals; unknown when a side has no level. The one producer of a book's depth ladder: the card's bars, totals, difference and sentence, and the microstructure's `depth` (carried by `_microstructure_structural`). |
| Divergence, venues disagree | `app.options.order_flow.engine.readout_card` | The weighted headline and the flat top five of one book lean strictly opposite ways ("Touch and deeper book disagree"); NYSE_BOOK's and NASDAQ_BOOK's headlines lean strictly opposite ways at the view shown (VENUES DISAGREE). A zero or unknown side is no disagreement. |
| Touch state | `app.options.order_flow.engine._readout` | LOCKED when a book's best bid equals its best offer, CROSSED when above; the composite's can be either though neither book is (two books taken apart in time). Crossed withholds the card's sentence; locked keeps it. |
| Spans, level counts | `app.options.order_flow.engine._readout` (`_span`) | Schwab sends each level's price, not its distance. A side's span is its touch price to its Nth level's (its last when it has fewer); the counts are the levels each side has, shown when a side has fewer than five. |
| Composite live | `server.liquidity_readout` | Live only while both books are by the one live rule; otherwise the word of the book that is not. A book's rule reads the daemon's held services, so a Nasdaq-listed ticker's NYSE_BOOK, subscribed and accepted though Schwab sends no levels for it, does not hold its composite back. |
| Book imbalance, weighted top 5 | `app.options.order_flow.engine._readout` (`OF_LEVEL_WEIGHTS`) | The same ratio with level k's shares weighted exp(−0.5·(k−1)), the five normalized to 1 (0.43, 0.26, 0.16, 0.10, 0.06: a vendor study's touch-weighted starting point, adjustable), renormalized over the levels a thin side has. The card's default headline. |
| Composite book | `app.options.order_flow.engine.book_readout` (`_composite_side`) | Schwab sends NYSE_BOOK and NASDAQ_BOOK apart, and NYSE_BOOK carries Nasdaq's own quote too (SPY 2026-09-25: one 767.39 level of 160 = ARCX 80 + NSDQ 80). Every quote of the books held for the ticker, one per contributor, codes matched without regard to case; a contributor in both books from the copy with the newer quote time Schwab sent on its row (market-maker field 2, schwab-py's SEQUENCE; on an equal time NYSE_BOOK's), as a bank takes the latest-dated statement of one account (operator 2026-11-04). On 2026-11-02 NSDQ was the only code in both, the two copies at different prices in 52% of sides and neither book always the newer. With one book held (a Nasdaq-listed ticker: Schwab sends NYSE_BOOK only for NYSE Arca's SPY and IWM), the composite is that book. The shares summed at each distinct price, best first. A level without per-exchange rows is taken whole and the dedup marked partial; a copy sent without its quote time is never newer than one sent with it (equal, or neither sent: NYSE_BOOK's), and when that contributor is in both books the dedup is marked partial. Its time is the older book's. |
| Smoothed headline, zone, held, flips, lean | `app.options.order_flow.engine.readout_card` over `history.LEAN` | One value a second per book and composite (the newest book's), the standing value carried through a second Schwab sent nothing. Headline: the median of the last 5 seconds. Zone: BALANCED under 10%, leaning to under 70%, very one-sided from 70% (a guide: the 10% line is Bookmap's colour switch, the 70% a vendor study). The zone shown changes once the headline has stood outside it 3 seconds in a row, whichever zones it crossed, to the zone it stands in then; held is the seconds since the headline entered that zone, shown as "at least" when it has stood there since the first value kept. Flips: the zone shown reaching the side opposite the one it last held, bid to offer or back, in 2 minutes (BALANCED is no side: bid, BALANCED, offer is one flip on arrival; the first lean is none; very one-sided counts as its side; an unknown value starts over); 5 or more CHOPPY (adjustable). Lean: the 2-minute and 5-minute headline series, buyers when all positive, sellers when all negative, else mixed (a window touching zero is mixed). |
| Auction windows | `time_et.auction` | Schwab's /markets sends the regular window, no auction. The 5 minutes before it opens and the 10 minutes before it ends (13:00 ET on an early close), as the exchanges run their opening and closing auctions. Inside them the card keeps its number and adds a line. |
| Stock market session | `time_et.session_label` | Schwab sends no session name. Its /markets answer gives the day's windows (pre-market, regular, post-market), and the label is the window `now` is in: Pre-Market, RTH, After-Hours, Closed outside them, Unknown with no answer held. Its pre-market window starts at 07:00 ET, while Schwab streams the stock trades made from 04:00 ET, so before the first window the label is Pre-Market from the first stock trade Schwab streamed that was made that day (`time_et.record_trade`, LEVELONE_EQUITIES TRADE_TIME_MILLIS). |
| Rail and spot geometry | the confluence page | The page places each served level price and the served spot between the rendered strike-row centers. That is the design spec's display geometry. It does not produce a price. |
| Value area against the prior day's | `liquidity_value_engine.value_area_relation` | Schwab sends no volume profile. Today's value area (its low and high, estimated from Schwab's 1-minute bars) against the prior day's, by the two areas' own edges and no tolerance: `higher` (wholly above), `lower` (wholly below), `inside`, `outside`, `overlapping_higher`, `overlapping_lower` (both edges moved that way), `unchanged`. Unknown without all four edges. The Market Profile day-to-day value comparison (operator 2026-11-11: the percent thresholds it replaces had no source). |
| VWAP against today's value area | `liquidity_value_engine.vwap_against_value` | Schwab sends no VWAP (neither of its two documents names one) and no value area. `above_value` over the area's high, `below_value` under its low, `inside_value` between them, edges included. Unknown without the VWAP or an edge. |
