=== Schwab Trader API Support, 2026-10-12 ===
Thank you for contacting Schwab Trader API Support!
Please see our answers below:
1. Time & Sales
No. There is not any Schwab Trader API endpoint or streamer service that provides a complete options Time & Sales feed containing every individual trade, price, size, and timestamp.

2. Trade Side / Aggressor Information
No. The Trader API does not provide fields indicating:

* Trade aggressor side (buyer-initiated vs. seller-initiated)
* Trade condition codes
* Opening vs. closing trade indicators
* Bid/ask execution side classification

3. Missing Trades on LEVELONE_OPTIONS
What you are observing is expected. LEVELONE_OPTIONS should be viewed as a quote stream using a change-based delivery model rather than a complete trade-event feed. The streamer updates approximately every 300 milliseconds to 1 second depending on activity and only transmits fields whose values have changed.
As a result, LAST_PRICE, LAST_SIZE, TRADE_TIME, and TOTAL_VOLUME should not be interpreted as a complete audit trail of every trade. There is not a supported method within Trader API to receive every individual options trade.

4. Symbol Count
We generally recommend remaining under 300 subscribed symbols for best performance, although LEVELONE services support up to 3,000 symbols.
However, reducing the symbol count should not be interpreted as a method of receiving every trade. Staying below 300 symbols may help reduce performance-related concerns, but LEVELONE_OPTIONS is still not designed to function as a complete trade-by-trade feed.

5. Open Interest
Open interest is not a real-time value. It reflects the prior trading day's open interest as reported after the previous session's close.

6. Volume Going Down
A decrease in TOTAL_VOLUME is uncommon but can occur. Trade corrections, cancellations, or exchange-side adjustments would be potential explanations.

7. OPTIONS_BOOK
OPTIONS_BOOK provides depth-of-book quote information. Market Maker IDs identify the participant and/or venue contributing the displayed quote at a particular bid or ask level.
The service reports:

* Bid prices
* Ask prices
* Quote sizes
* Market maker participation at each level

It does not provide:

* Executed trade records
* Trade prices and sizes
* Aggressor side information
* Opening/closing trade indicators
* Execution reports

In other words, OPTIONS_BOOK should be viewed as an order-book depth service rather than a trade-reporting service.
There is not a documented method that Schwab recommends for determining trade side or aggressor side from the fields provided by the Trader API. Since the API does not provide trade-side indicators, aggressor information, or trade condition data, any methodology used to infer buyer- or seller-initiated activity would be an application-level interpretation rather than a value supplied or endorsed by Schwab. As such, we do not have a recommended approach for deriving trade side from the available API fields.
