## Confluence matrix (operator 2026-10-18)

The matrix ranks one expiry of the chain already priced in memory. It does not read the chain
database. The page asks again when the console pushes `levels` or `chain`. Flow and the change in
gamma stay dashes and their weights stay at zero. The written starting weights stay frozen. The
weekly distance check that would later move a weight is not part of this.

A negative gamma leg leaves that factor missing. When open interest plus volume weights sum to
zero, the volatility blend is the simple mean of the contracts that have a volatility. The header
chip marks a price not live. The matrix's line is the levels' one verdict (`push.verdict.terrain_staleness`:
LIVE, CLOSE, STALE or NOT LIVE), the chain's time and age, and why when not LIVE or CLOSE (AGENTS
rule 5; `tests/test_data_path_confluence_v1.py`). The window is every per-strike panel's (`docs/DATA_FLOW.md` "Strike windows", Auto:
eleven strikes centred on the one nearest the live price; with no live price, the middle of the
chain and the note saying so), chosen at each request from every strike the publication ranked,
so it follows the price between chains (operator 2026-10-29: "for the confluence matrix it is not
shifting so that we have 5+/- strikes around spot"; asked whether the matrix uses the same strike
window as the heatmap and the other panels: "Yes, one window"). The strike column shows two
decimals. Percentiles use the whole expiry. Each level is drawn at its own price on the rail.
A price outside the visible strikes is a button above or below the matrix. The page's placement
of those served prices, and of the spot line, is display geometry from the design spec. It is
not a second price. The expiry, including
0DTE, is chosen from a dropdown. With no priced chain, the page says so. A selected strike may name
the missing input. Charm
for the chosen expiry uses that expiry's contracts at the book's valuation time. Known open
interest of zero is zero charm.

Outside a ticker's option window, and on a day Schwab says is closed, the close values are the
balance until the next open (`docs/DATA_FLOW.md` §3.4, the option chain): Schwab's values as of
the close, a reported 0 included, and nothing sent after them replaces them. This replaces the
2026-10-22/25 rule that a reported zero outside the window kept the last count above zero.
