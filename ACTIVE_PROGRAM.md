## Confluence matrix (operator 2026-10-18)

The matrix ranks one expiry of the chain already priced in memory. It does not read the chain
database. The page asks again when the console pushes `levels` or `chain`. Flow and the change in
gamma stay dashes and their weights stay at zero. The written starting weights stay frozen. The
weekly distance check that would later move a weight is not part of this.

A negative gamma leg leaves that factor missing. When open interest plus volume weights sum to
zero, the volatility blend is the simple mean of the contracts that have a volatility. The header
chip is the only not-live mark. The matrix shows the chain's time. It does not show chain age or a
STALE word. The window is five strikes below the price and five above, plus a strike on the
price. The strike column shows two decimals. Percentiles use the whole expiry. Each level is drawn at its own price on the rail.
A price outside the visible strikes is a button above or below the matrix. The page's placement
of those served prices, and of the spot line, is display geometry from the design spec. It is
not a second price. The expiry, including
0DTE, is chosen from a dropdown. With no priced chain, the page says so. A selected strike may name the missing input. Charm
for the chosen expiry uses that expiry's contracts at the book's valuation time. Known open
interest of zero is zero charm.

Outside a session day's option window (operator 2026-10-22: the last number stands after the
close, the way a balance stands after the bank closes), a reported open interest or volume of
zero leaves the last count above zero. That count is the one already published, or the newest
stored capture when this process has not published one. A missing count and Schwab's no-value
code are not that zero. A day with no option window keeps a reported zero (operator
2026-09-27: open interest 0 is 0). A chain begun inside the window keeps a reported zero.
A streamed zero after the close does not replace the count. A zero book from a closed day
does not hide the stored capture before the next open.
