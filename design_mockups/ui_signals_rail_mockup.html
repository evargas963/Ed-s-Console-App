Signals Rail — design mockup
Vertical analytics column · right of horizon row + signal-rail-card

New SIGNALS rail sits to the right of the 4-pill horizon row + signal-rail-card. Each slot is a self-contained alert/context surface for an institutional signal pulled from anywhere in the repo. Same severity grammar as Entry armed / Stack degraded — dim → amber → red (positive signals get green). Slots hide when fully quiet (no real-estate cost when nothing's happening).

Starting with the Level Test slot at the top. Other slots in this mockup are stubs showing what the same visual contract looks like for additional signals — they're not wired yet; you pick which ones plug in over time.

Design notes
  1. Severity grammar matches signal-rail-card — quiet (slate) → building (amber) → hot (red) → positive (green). Operator already learned this from Entry armed / Stack degraded; no new vocabulary.
  2. Slots hide when quiet for low-priority signals (or stay compact for high-priority context). No always-on noise.
  3. Severity pip in slot header gives an at-a-glance summary even if the operator's eyes are scanning vertically.
  4. Rail header carries summary count ("1 hot · 1 building · 2 quiet") — operator can tell at a glance whether to look right or stay on the main row.
  5. Extensible — adding a new signal = adding a new .signal-slot + a render call. No structural changes.
