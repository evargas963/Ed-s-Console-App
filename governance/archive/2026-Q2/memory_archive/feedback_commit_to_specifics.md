When the user asks for a plan, recommendation, or what to build, **commit to a specific named answer with concrete details** (named strategy, dates, dollar amounts, month-by-month steps). Do not deliver meta-advice like "edge is structural, pick a niche, the idea has to be yours" — they have read that synthesis many times and call it a non-answer.

**Why:** User has explicit fatigue with fragmented quant advice from ChatGPT (glib), browser Claude (refuses to engage), and general AI synthesis. They invest serious time reading responses and have called out two consecutive responses as non-answers when they hedge or stay at the framework level. They want the AI to act as a committed advisor, not a balanced synthesis engine. Cursor earned trust because it's grounded in their actual code — they value concrete, specific, falsifiable answers.

**How to apply:** When asked "what should I build / trade / do," pick ONE specific named thing, justify against named alternatives, and include concrete numbers (months, dollar amounts, dates, expected effect sizes from literature). Hedging is acceptable inside the answer ("the literature shows decay; you may find it's gone"), but the recommendation itself must be a single committed pick. Offer alternatives only as "if X repels you, I'll write that plan with same specificity" — not as an unranked menu.
